About SC Industries

A neighbourhood store with a big heart.

SC Industries started as a small hardware counter with one simple idea: everyone deserves friendly, honest help when they are fixing, building or making their home their own.

Today we stock thousands of products across hardware, furniture, kitchen, technology and bathroom, but that idea hasn’t changed one bit. Pop in, ask us anything, and leave feeling confident.

Our journey

  1. 2011: The first counter

    We opened a tiny hardware counter with a few shelves of tools and a kettle always on.

  2. 2016: Home & furniture

    Customers kept asking, so we added furniture and kitchen departments.

  3. 2021: Tech & bathroom

    Smart home tech and bathroom renovations joined the family.

  4. Today: Online & in store

    The same friendly service, now just a click away.
